Privacy Policy for Bluetooth Keyboard for PC

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

Application: Bluetooth Keyboard for PC (Package: com.smart.bluetooth.keyboard)

Summary: This app operates strictly as a local input peripheral (Bluetooth HID and local Wi-Fi bridge). It does NOT collect, record, log, store, or transmit your keystrokes, personal information, or device telemetry to any remote server or third party.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Keystroke Logging: Any text or code entered into the app is converted locally into USB/Bluetooth HID scancodes and transmitted directly to your paired computer. We do not store or monitor what you type.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require registration, accounts, names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Location Data: The app utilizes the neverForLocation Bluetooth flag on Android 12+. We do not track, derive, or access your physical or GPS location.

2. Permissions Used and Why

  • Bluetooth & Nearby Devices (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Required strictly to establish direct Bluetooth Human Interface Device (HID) pairing and low-latency communication with your computer or ESP32 bridge.
  • Foreground Service (Connected Device): Used to keep the Bluetooth connection alive when you switch apps or lock the screen, ensuring that keys are not dropped and repeating-key errors do not occur.
  • Local Wi-Fi (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively to communicate over your local private Wi-Fi network with your ESP32 Wi-Fi module when ESP32 Mode is selected. No internet data is sent externally.
  • Wake Lock: Prevents the CPU from sleeping during an active typing session so keystrokes stream without latency.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose any user data to third parties, analytics providers, or advertising networks.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the support email listed on our Google Play Store page.
